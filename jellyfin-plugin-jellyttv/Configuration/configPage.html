JellyTTV

Configure the connection to your JellyTTV backend. The plugin will fetch live Twitch streams and display them in Jellyfin's sidebar and home screen.

Connection

The base URL of your JellyTTV instance as reachable from Jellyfin.
Only needed if your JellyTTV instance requires authentication.

Display Options

How often to poll JellyTTV for live channel updates (10-600 seconds).
